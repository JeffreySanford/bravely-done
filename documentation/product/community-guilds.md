# Community, Parties, and Guilds

Community unlocks after the player learns the solo loop. It should create support, belonging, and cooperative momentum rather than competition anxiety.

## Progression

1. Trusted allies
2. Temporary party
3. Shared quests
4. Persistent guild
5. Cooperative expeditions and campaigns

## Cooperative mechanics

- Different party members complete different encounters toward one shared objective.
- Team success grants collective construction, story, or cosmetic progress.
- Help, encouragement, and unblocking earn reputation.
- Privacy controls determine which quest details, schedules, and results are shared.

## Safety requirements

- Block, mute, report, and leave controls
- Age-appropriate community boundaries
- Moderation evidence and appeals process
- No forced public productivity scores
- No ability for guild leaders to access private personal tasks without explicit sharing
